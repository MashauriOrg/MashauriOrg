What is a Mashauri Virtual Incubator

The Mashauri Virtual Incubator is an organisation-facing, cohort-based programme that guides students through part of the entrepreneurial journey. For instance the validation of a problem and solution. Our incubators are built on a series of sprints inside a 4 week period. Each incubator maps onto part of the entrepreeurial journey based on our Startup Spiral concept.

Delivery combines weekly sprints, practical assignments, webinars, mentor support, and AI-assisted feedback. Incubator leaders receive reporting on engagement, progression, completion, and at-risk students, with clear artefacts to evaluate programme impact.

Implementation workflow

1
Apply & select
Students apply via short forms; cohorts are selected to match institutional capacity and goals.
2
Onboarding & pre-work
Pre-pilot webinar and readiness checks align participant expectations and baseline skills.
3
Weekly sprints
Micro-lessons, quizzes, assignments, and peer learning released in predictable weekly cycles.
4
Mentor + AI coaching
Mentor office hours plus AI rubric feedback improve quality and iteration speed (incubator dependent).
5
Assessment, certification & progression
Teams finish with validated models/pitches and receive recommendations for next programme pathways.

Who is this for?

Universities, innovation offices, and incubator centres that need scalable venture education with measurable outcomes.

How much university staff time is needed?

A project manager/focal point usually coordinates recruitment and reporting while Mashauri supports day-to-day facilitation.

How is evidence captured?

Each team builds an evidence trail: interview notes, experiments, scorecards, and final pitch materials.

A day in the life — student perspective

“My day in the Mashauri incubator”

I check the leaderboard and my weekly module, complete a short lesson and quiz, run customer interviews, upload notes to my project drive, and use AI self-checks to improve evidence quality. I finish with assignment upload, peer feedback, and mentor/webinar support.
